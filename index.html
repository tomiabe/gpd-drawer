This is a true-blue Aussie style of Shiraz to have with your peppered steak fresh off the barbie. Smoky, charred meat notes with black pepper and plenty of dark plums bursting through.

Shiraz does well across climates. Warm regions give you a luscious, fruity glass, cooler ones lean savoury and peppery. Both are good drinking, so it comes down to taste.

Design principle

What I kept: the overlay over the grid, the in-drawer add to cart, and the taste and pairing content. Those are the parts already pulling their weight. What changed is the order and the emphasis. This is a sketch to react to, not a spec. The taste meter positions are my read of the panel wording (medium body, low sweetness, low acidity, some fruitiness, medium tannins, some oak), and the bottle is a drawn stand-in because I did not have the real product image.
